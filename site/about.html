About Arda

An Abuja-based advisory firm serving governments, development institutions, businesses and the aviation industry.

The firm

Arda Limited advises on strategy, delivery, safety and compliance through two practices: development and enterprise advisory, and aviation advisory.

The development and enterprise practice joins public sector economics and national planning experience to commercial strategy for early-stage companies in fintech, agri-tech and deep-tech. For governments and development institutions it covers policy research, programme design, delivery, and monitoring and evaluation. For businesses it covers go-to-market strategy, financial modelling, investment readiness and impact reporting.

The aviation practice joins aeronautical engineering training to regulatory inspection experience and hands-on leadership of safety, quality and compliance functions. It supports operators, airports, air navigation service providers and regulators with safety and quality management systems, certification, audits, regulatory development and operational efficiency.

How we work

Four commitments on every assignment

We work from experience

Our competencies were built inside the kinds of organisations we advise: federal government planning, a civil aviation authority, an aircraft operator's safety and quality functions, and early-stage ventures.

We work to a standard

Aviation assignments start from the applicable framework. Development assignments start from the result the programme is meant to achieve. Each gives the client something to check our work against.

We work from evidence

Financial models, market sizing, Power BI, Python and AI workflow automation are everyday tools for us. In aviation the same habit shows up as data-driven safety management.

We stay through delivery

Our involvement continues after the recommendations. Programme administration, project management, system implementation and audit are all services we offer.
